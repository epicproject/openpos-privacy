Open POS

Privacy Policy · Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: Open POS has no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours that stores your sales. Everything it records — products, carts, prices, takings — lives on your own counter PC. We never receive it, and there is no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind in either app.

What this covers

Open POS is a point-of-sale system in two halves, and this policy covers both:

  • Open POS — the Windows program that runs on the shop's counter PC.
  • Open POS Scanner — the Android app that turns a phone into the barcode scanner for that counter.

What we collect

Nothing. Neither app contains an analytics SDK, a crash reporting service, an advertising network, or a third-party tracker. We do not hold a copy of your products, your prices, your sales, or your customers.

The two ways the phone reaches the counter

Offline (LAN) — the default. The phone talks straight to your own PC over your own wifi. Nothing leaves the shop's network, and the internet is not involved at all.

Online (Cloud) — optional, for a phone on mobile data or a different network. The messages travel through a relay server, which pairs the two ends and passes the frames between them. The relay does not open, store or log the contents of a bill; it holds only the short-lived pairing code and the two live connections, and forgets both when the connection closes. The source of the relay ships with the product, so a shop can run its own — in which case nothing passes through us at all.

Permissions, and why each one is asked for

  • Camera — to read barcodes. The preview is on screen while it is in use.
  • Internet / network state / wifi state — to reach the counter PC, and to notice when the connection drops so the queue can take over.
  • Multicast — to find the counter on the shop's wifi without anyone typing an IP address.
  • Vibrate — the buzz that confirms a scan.

The app asks for no contacts, no location, no files, no microphone, and no phone identity.

The Windows program

It stores its catalogue, carts and sales in files on that PC, under your own Windows user account. It does not send them anywhere. It opens a port on the local network so the phone can connect, and advertises itself over mDNS and a small UDP broadcast so the phone can find it. A phone may only join by presenting the pairing token shown on your own screen — being on the same wifi is not, by itself, permission to join the till.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics.
  • We have no access to your PC, your phone, or your shop's records.

Children

Open POS is a tool for running a shop. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Android app removes everything it held, including the pending queue. Your sales live on the counter PC; deleting them there deletes them, because there is no other copy.
